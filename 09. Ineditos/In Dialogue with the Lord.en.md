# FOREWORD {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.presentacion}

My beloved daughters and sons:

Our Father, who strove heroically to follow our Lord’s footsteps, liked to use parables and comparisons in his preaching to help us understand and assimilate the good doctrine he gave us. He often referred to the gold coins which, according to the ancient chronicles, kings used to distribute on special occasions, explaining that he too always tried to do the same, in the presence of God. Conscious of the divine treasure our Lord had given him, he strove constantly to offer his children the gold coins of the spirit of Opus Dei.

In words which Holy Scripture applies to the great prophet Elijah, we too can say that the words of our holy Founder arose like a fire (Sir 48:1). They illuminated our minds, they inflamed our hearts, and they filled us with optimism and fortitude in our battle for the glory of God and the salvation of souls. And so we cherished them in our memory, we considered them time and again in our 14 FOREWORD

prayer, and they inspired us to deepen in the divine treasure which our Founder scattered with both hands. They helped us so much that, right from the start, brothers of ours took care to note them down, and in our Father’s later years, to record them on tape.

I can appreciate, therefore, how happy you are to receive this book, which fulfils the often-expressed desire to produce in a single volume some texts of our Father’s oral preaching already published in Crónica and Noticias; for the daughters and sons of God in Opus Dei, it is a real treasure to have. Don Alvaro, though he looked forward to seeing it done, didn’t see it finished in his lifetime. I’m sending it to you now this year in which we celebrate the twentieth anniversary of our holy Founder’s dies natalis.

In unison with our Father’s prayer (his conversation with his daughters and sons was always prayer) we can enter a little into the depths of his relationship with God. May it serve each of us as a channel to progress, day by day, along the pathways of the interior life! This was our beloved Father’s only objective whenever he gave himself joyfully to the task of providing us with the spiritual nourishment our souls needed.

This collection of meditations retains all the spontaneity of the charming, direct and profoundly evangelical style so characteristic of our Founder. They span a period of twenty years: starting in November 1954 and finishing on 27 March 1975, the eve of the golden jubilee of his ordination, just a few months before he went to heaven. There is a wonderful continuity in the spiritual teachings they contain. The supernatural light which illuminated our Founder’s life on 2 October 1928 thereafter filled his preaching and his earthly journey, and grew more and more intense without ever ceasing to be relevant, because it was founded on the perennial relevance of the Gospel.

## With Christ, in Peter’s boat {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.1.1}

When you do your prayer, my son – and I am not referring now to that constant prayer that spans the whole day, but rather to those two periods when we withdraw from external affairs and concentrate exclusively on talking to God – when you begin your meditation, frequently, depending on the circumstances, you call to mind the scene or the mystery you want to contemplate. Then you apply your understanding and you immediately begin to talk to our Lord affectionately, full of love and sorrow, of thanksgiving and of desires to improve. If you follow this path you should come to a prayer of quiet in which it is our Lord who speaks, and you listen to whatever he has to say. How well you notice then those interior motions and reprimands that set the soul on fire! To facilitate prayer, we should “materialize” even the most spiritual things, as the parables do. This teaching is divine. Doctrine must reach our mind and our heart by way of the senses: now you see why I like to talk to you so much about boats and seas. We have climbed into Peter’s boat along with Christ, into this boat of the Church, which seems so fragile and rickety but which no storm can ever sink. And while we are here in Peter’s boat, you and I must consider slowly, slowly: Lord, why am I in this boat? This question has a special meaning for you since the moment you entered the boat, this boat of Opus Dei. You came because you wanted to, and that for me is the most supernatural of reasons. I love you, Lord, because I wish to love you. I could have given this poor heart to some human being... but no! I place it whole, young, vibrant, noble and clean, at your feet, because I want to!

## Contemplative and serene {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.1.4}

My son, don’t ever think about yourself. Don’t be so proud as to imagine that you are what in my country is called el palito de la gaita, the centre of attraction. As long as you don’t think about yourself you will do good work. We shouldn’t believe that we are the centre of the universe and that everything ought to revolve around us. And the worst thing is that if you fall into this defect, when someone tells you that you’re proud you won’t believe it. For while the humble person thinks he is proud, the proud person thinks he is humble. I look upon you, my sons... How wonderful it will be when the moment comes for you to teach your brothers that the children of God in his Opus Dei have to be contemplatives, contemplative souls in the middle of the world. You have to maintain a constant life of prayer, from morning till night and from night till morning. From night till morning, Father? Yes, my son, even while you’re asleep. Just like me, you admire the silent life of those men who enclose themselves in an old monastery, hidden in their cells, with their life of work and prayer. Whenever I visited the Carthusians, I left very edified and loving them very much. I understand their vocation, their separation from the world, and I am happy for them, but... there, inside I feel a great sadness. And when I emerge once more into the street I tell myself: my cell, this is my cell! Our life is as contemplative as theirs. God makes it possible that our cell, our place of retreat and recollection, is in the middle of the things of the world, in the interior of our hearts. And if we have acquired our specific formation, we will spend the day in constant conversation with God. Christ, Mary, the Church. Three loves to fill a life. # THE PRAYER OF THE CHILDREN OF GOD * {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.2}

## The period of the ‘premonitions’ {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.7.2}

As time went by there came those first manifestations of our Lord’s action: those inklings about something, something he wanted of me. This for me was to actually touch the grace of God: I saw it as God’s doing, something I hadn’t expected. My father died exhausted. He had a smile on his lips and a special lovable quality. I like to compare our soul to a vessel which God our Lord has made to store the liqueur of Wisdom, a gift, a very great grace of the Holy Spirit. My daughters and sons, wisdom is an aura of the might of God and a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty; therefore nothing sullied enters into her. For she is the splendour of eternal light, the spotless mirror of the power of God, the image of his goodness. And she, who is one, can do all things, and renews everything while herself enduring; and passes into holy souls from age to age.1

## A divine treasure in vessels of clay {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.18.2}

The important thing is personal holiness, my daughters and sons. It is the one thing necessary.6 Wisdom consists in knowing God and loving him. And so that you may never be caught by surprise, I will remind you with St Paul: Habemus autem thesaurum istum in vasis fictilibus:7 we carry this treasure in vessels of clay, fragile and easily broken, ut sublimitas sit virtutis Dei et non ex nobis,8 so that it is obvious that all the beauty and all the power we contain come from God and not from us. Holy Scripture also says that the heart of a fool is like a broken vessel which lets wisdom escape.9 With these words the Holy Spirit teaches us not to be childish or foolish. As children of God we must be strong. We carry out our work and our professional tasks constantly in God’s presence, and that enables us to aim for perfection in little things. We must keep our vessel intact so that the divine liqueur is not lost. If we direct everything to God, including our passions, then the vessel will not break. I have been through so much, and I feel I’m dreaming when I contemplate the magnificent reality of our Opus Dei and experience my children’s loyalty to God, to the Church and to the Work. It is not surprising that from time to time somebody falls by the way. We give everyone the right nourishment, but there is no guarantee that even a well-balanced diet will be perfectly digested. This misfortune could befall any of us, my daughters and sons; it could also happen to me. As long as I am on earth I too am capable of doing something terribly stupid. ## How to be loyal {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.18.4}

Be faithful, be loyal! You will have many opportunities in life to be unfaithful and disloyal, because we are not hothouse plants. We are exposed to cold and heat, to snow and storms. We are like trees exposed to the four winds: at times they get covered with dust, but 170 IN DIALOGUE WITH THE LORD

they become clean and beautiful when washed by the rain of God’s grace. Don’t be surprised by anything. I repeat, if you are not proud, you will always persevere! And what will you do to respond to the love of this beautiful Mother of ours, the Work? What must you do to be loyal? The answer is simple, very simple. In the first place you must let yourself be guided, without protest, because you don’t know yourselves. I am more than seventy years old and I still don’t know myself well, so how far will your self-knowledge get you? In my case I don’t get ‘fraternal’ corrections, but two of your brothers tell me clearly whatever they consider opportune, and I’m very grateful for it. Practise fraternal correction! It is a marvellous way of loving one another, if you observe the precautions to avoid making it unpleasant. It is unpleasant for the person who makes it; but the person who receives it should be grateful, just as one should be grateful to a doctor for lancing an infected wound in order to heal it. Then, set to work. I have told you countless times that no one loses his personality when he comes to the Work. Diversity, a healthy pluralism, is a sign of good spirit. So act on your own; no one will stop you from doing so. Opus Dei fully respects the personality of each of her children. Without losing our freedom we relinquish it, because we want to. This is the most supernatural of reasons: because we want to, because of love.

## Unity of life {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.18.5}

Be noble, upright people by reason of your struggle, your endeavour to reconcile the two brothers we all carry within: our intellect, guided by God’s grace, and our sensuality. These two brothers are with us from birth and accompany us throughout life. We must get them to live together even though they tug in different directions. We must endeavour to have the superior one, the THE LIQUEUR OF WISDOM 171

intellect, guide the lesser one, the senses. Our soul, following the dictates of faith and reason aided by God’s grace, aspires to the greatest of gifts: paradise, eternal happiness. And we must also direct there our little brother, sensuality, so that he may enjoy God in heaven. May this unity of life be the result of God’s goodness towards each of us and towards the Work and also the result of our personal struggle. There is no better time than now to recall that peace is the consequence of war: of this marvellous war against ourselves, against our evil inclinations. This war is a war of peace, because it seeks peace. We lose our serenity when it is not our intellect, with divine grace, that directs our life but rather our evil inclinations. Don’t be surprised to find you are like monsters, capable of committing the greatest atrocities! With God’s help we will reach the end safely, with the peace that is – I repeat – a consequence of victory. The victory is not ours, though, because God is the one who wins out in us if we don’t create difficulties and if we make the effort to stretch out our hand and grasp the one that is offered us from heaven. My children, seek unity of life. Struggle. Don’t let the vessel I talked about ever break. Let your heart be whole and let it be for God. Let us not get tripped up by the miseries of personal pride. Let us really give ourselves. Let us keep going, like someone walking towards a city, making certain to keep taking one step after another until we reach the end. We will never lack the help of our Father God. My greatest joy in life is to know that you are struggling and that you are loyal. It doesn’t bother me too much to hear that you have fallen flat on your face in things that are far from the main battlements. I know that you will get up and begin again more determinedly. Perhaps we will lose a battle, but we will win the war. And if we are sincere, we don’t even lose the lost battles. ## Far from the battlements of the castle {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.18.6}

Do you know what I usually do? Like a good general I plan the fight for the vanguard, far from the fortress, on little fronts here and there. I really love to receive the blessing of other priests, and I use these blessings as a wall to protect me. I also have to struggle; and I try to struggle where it suits me best: far away, in things which in themselves are not very important, which are not even faults if they are not fulfilled. Each person should carry on his struggle on his own front line, with holy shrewdness. As long as we hold to the certainty of the entire faith of Christ and we struggle, our Lord will give us his grace abundantly and will continue to bless us: with sufferings – which we will always have, but don’t exaggerate them, because they are ordinarily small – with abundant vocations all over the world, and with the growth of apostolic works and undertakings that demand so much work and so much spirit of sacrifice. And all this, without counting the most beautiful part of our work, which is what each one of you does on his or her own personal initiative. For the children of God in his Opus Dei are light and fire, and often a bonfire. They are burning coals; they are leaven that ferments everything around them. Let us not be filled with pride or arrogance, even though the contrast with other poor people is so evident. Let us thank our Lord for everything, knowing that none of it is ours. God gives it to us because he wants to; he also sends us his grace: clear light to help us fight. Hence, in spite of our frailties, imperfections and mistakes, let us not go off the road, let us never break the vessel that the Holy Spirit in his mercy has chosen to fill with wisdom and goodness.

## The first Residence {#inedito.in-dialogue-with-the-lord.22.3}

This house is coming along very nicely, isn’t it? You need to realise what good care God in his providence has taken of us, like a father and a mother. At the beginning of the Work I thought, and I wrote, that in no way would there be women in Opus Dei. And so I began to make arrangements for the catering services in our Centres. I was looking for some type of vocation that would do... Not lay brothers, because we couldn’t have monks; it had to be something else. Oh Lord, what a disaster! Each attempt was worse than the last. Afterwards we got some ladies to do the cooking, but that didn’t work either. I then got a chef. The corporate apostolates came later. The corporate apostolates are not the essential element of the Work: the essential idea is that each individual lives unattached wherever he is, and behaves as a son of God at all times, and lives for Love, and works for Love, and feels himself always sustained by the divine strength of Love. Now, where was I? Yes, the first meal in the first Residence, which was not the first corporate apostolate. The first course was rice a la cubana, which is white rice with fried sliced banana.
