## INTRODUCTORY NOTE

From the first appearances of Crónica and Noticias, each issue carried a few incisive phrases by our Father on its front pages. These passages were first collected and published in book form fifteen years ago, in January 1981. Since then we, his daughters and sons, have often used them as material for our prayer, and they have inspired us to put into practice, with the help of God’s grace, an aspiration of his which sums up all our desires: to do Opus Dei on earth, being you yourself Opus Dei.1

This second edition includes some additional material which originally appeared in the “Hoja Informativa” (the predecessor of Crónica and

1 From our Father, prologue to the Catechism of the Work, 23 April 1947

Noticias and successor to the “Noticias” which our Father used to send to the students in contact with the Ferraz residence, and later during the Spanish Civil War) as well as other excerpts from manuscript notes by our Founder, from among the many we have.

The title was chosen by Don Alvaro when he asked for the new edition to be prepared, some years before he went to our heavenly home. It describes its content very well, because our Father’s objective, in sending these phrases to the printers every month, was to provide us with a starting-point for our prayer. He always used to say that there are many ways to travel along the path of the Work – even hopping on one leg, he would add jokingly – as long as one didn’t leave the “motorway” which our Lord has provided for us and for many other souls in the Church. And, in sharing with us the treasures he had in his heart, he offered us what he used to refer to as the guidelines on which, under the impulse of the Holy Spirit, each of us should write the story of our own interior life. Our Father wanted each of his daughters and sons, “alone with God”, to tease out the spiritual content of the considerations he offered us from the intimacy of his relationship with Christ.

In his short introduction to the first edition, Don Alvaro described these considerations of our Founder as “gold coins” which we should always cherish as a treasure. We ask him to intercede for us all, so that we may learn to listen to, take in, and meditate on our beloved and holy Founder’s words

with the same receptiveness, fidelity and profit which always shone out in his own life. your Father + Javier Rome, 9 January 1996 Anniversary of Blessed Josemaría’s birth

## PROLOGUE

Those of us who received from God the grace of living with our beloved Father, heard him say on more than one occasion that in carrying out the mission our Lord gave him, he always did what was done at the wedding of a king. He was referring to the largesse which the rulers of olden days used to bestow on their subjects: on their wedding day they were said to distribute gold coins to the populace so that everybody could enjoy their wealth.

Through his words and deeds our Father continually scattered gold coins in our souls. Like the faithful and wise servant of the parable, he drew forth, from the treasure God had given him, new things and old, as old as the Gospel,

and like the Gospel, new, to bring us closer to God.

From the start of our internal publications it was customary to have some words from our Father on the front page of each issue. These counsels form a collection of valuable coins with which we should trade. For years they have been the subject of our meditation and a source of firm resolutions, because we knew that they came from our Founder’s soul. And so they united us more closely to his heart, his spirit, and his intentions.

Now, five years after he went to heaven, I have concluded that you would all be very keen to have these phrases available in a single volume, so that we would be able to use them more easily to nourish our dialogue with God and to underpin deeply our self-giving in the service of souls.

I’m sure, my daughter, my son, that the same will happen to you as our beloved Father wrote in one of the points: When you bring to your prayer the things I have been telling you, you will realise that our Lord has chosen you with tremendous love and affection... (no. 12). And you will feel strengthened and full of gratitude, a gratitude that is expressed in the effective desire to be more Opus Dei; that is, better children of our Father, completely faithful ALONE WITH GOD 15 to the treasure of doctrine and life he has bequeathed us.

Alvaro Rome, 1 January 1981

## VOCATION

### 1 {#inedito.alone-with-god.1}

Don’t forget, my children, that we have a divine vocation. With this conviction, we can always be sure of persevering.

### 2 {#inedito.alone-with-god.2}

Redemi te et vocavi te nomine tuo; meus es tu (Is 43:1): I have redeemed you and called you by your name: you are mine. Vocation is always supernatural, a call from God. It ought also to be untouchable, virginal. And, in Opus Dei, cheerful.

### 3 {#inedito.alone-with-god.3}

All vocations are equally easy and equally difficult,

because God offers everybody his grace, and expects everybody to respond generously and completely.

### 4 {#inedito.alone-with-god.4}

You should feel the responsibility of your vocation to the Work: you have been chosen by God, with special love.

### 5 {#inedito.alone-with-god.5}

Our place is in the street. We live in the middle of society, among our equals, among all types of people, our arms outstretched to all souls, being light and salt, not wearing any kind of badge, since we have no need to. The only difference is on the inside, in our souls: the vocation our Lord has given us.

### 6 {#inedito.alone-with-god.6}

Thank our Lord for your vocation in practical ways: by finding out how to work hard, pray a lot, and always be concerned about other people. And then, at the end of the day, place all that effort at Jesus’ feet, with purity of intention.

### 7 {#inedito.alone-with-god.7}

All vocations are perfect because, if the soul is faithful, they can all lead to the most perfect holiness.

### 8 {#inedito.alone-with-god.8}

How beautiful our vocation is: it gives us here on earth a joy and peace the world cannot give! We can only lose that peace when we consent to things that lead us astray.

### 9 {#inedito.alone-with-god.9}

I recommend those who wish to respond to their calling to appeal to the Holy Spirit, begging his help in a special way by making a triduum. Do the same yourself.

### 10 {#inedito.alone-with-god.10}

God is your Father; he loved you and chose you from eternity. How can you possibly not respond to love like that

with an affectionate greeting and trusting, filial conversation every day?

### 11 {#inedito.alone-with-god.11}

How glad you will be that you have said no to other

loves, for Love! When this life is over, you will find Love in all its beauty and greatness, in all its splendour, colour and harmony.

### 12 {#inedito.alone-with-god.12}

When you bring to your prayer the things I have been

telling you, you will realise that our Lord has chosen you with tremendous love and affection. To each of you he has said: ego redemi te, I have redeemed you. Et vocavi te nomine tuo, and I have called you by your name; perhaps by your “nomignolo”, as they say in Italy, your pet name. And he then adds that wonderful phrase: meus es tu! You are mine! These considerations aren’t just idle fancy, my children, but a proof of the great love God has for us. They are words from Sacred Scripture, and they have to be understood for what they are: consoling, divine, wonderful truths.

## THE WORK

### 13 {#inedito.alone-with-god.13}

Many ways lead to God. In fact, they all do. Any state and any job in the world, provided it is honest and continues to be so, can be an encounter with God. To make people realise this wonderful fact, our Lord raised up his Opus Dei; and so, since 2nd October 1928, we have been telling all souls, by word – doctrine – and example that the divine paths of the earth have been opened up.

### 14 {#inedito.alone-with-god.14}

Opus Dei is an apostolic enterprise. It only sees souls. Opus Dei is not the tool of human ambitions. It belongs to God and the Church.

### 15 {#inedito.alone-with-god.15}

There are people who speak about the spirit of Opus Dei respectfully and affectionately, but who at times, on seeing the

ecumenical reality of our apostolates, speculate as to the means at our disposal. The means are as spiritual as the ends. Opus Dei can be described as an entity with spiritual and apostolic ends, achieved by equally spiritual means. Opus Dei does not have a double life, and its members try not to either. If a member of Opus Dei were ever to have anything approaching a double life, he or she would have the wrong spirit and would be unable to persevere in his or her apostolic work.

### 16 {#inedito.alone-with-god.16}

If Opus Dei had ever got involved in politics, even for a

second, I would have left the Work at that very moment. So don’t ever give any credence to anything which tries to link the Work with politics, or with temporal issues of any sort. For on the one hand, our means and aims are always exclusively supernatural; and on the other hand, everybody in the Work respects the fact that every single member, man or woman, is completely free in secular affairs, and as a logical consequence is personally responsible for his or her actions. Therefore it is impossible for Opus Dei ever to take a hand in any projects other than directly spiritual and apostolic ones, which can have nothing to do with any country’s politics.

### 17 {#inedito.alone-with-god.17}

This is your way: forever salt, forever light, forever joy,

forever fire, forever peace.

### 18 {#inedito.alone-with-god.18}

The children of God in Opus Dei have three ruling

passions: to hand on the doctrine of the faith; to guide in one way or another the souls who draw close to the warmth of our apostolate; and to love the unity of our Work.

### 19 {#inedito.alone-with-god.19}

Think about those birds that fly swiftly and majestically

to great heights, further than the eye can see. Though their wings are huge and heavy, they don’t feel their weight. If they were cut off, if the birds could be rid of the weight, they would be much lighter, but they would be unable to fly: they would crash to the ground. The same applies to our obligations in Opus Dei: they don’t weigh, they’re not negative; they are a continual “yes” of genuine love. By fulfilling them faithfully we soar to great heights. And, though personally we are worth so little, we live the life of God, we soar like eagles above the highest peaks, right up to the sun, and look it in the face.

### 20 {#inedito.alone-with-god.20}

Do you want an effective plan? I will give you this one,

which has been well tested in our Opus Dei: keep quiet, pray, work, and smile.

### 21 {#inedito.alone-with-god.21}

I love your parents very much, and I pray for them at

least twice a day. You too should hold the parents of your brothers and sisters in great, unselfish affection.

### 22 {#inedito.alone-with-god.22}

I pray for my children’s parents every day, and I love

them even though I’ve never met them. I pray for those our Lord has already taken, and for those who are still alive, helping us to be faithful by their prayer and example.

### 23 {#inedito.alone-with-god.23}

When you put your family and relations in contact with

the Work you are doing them a lot of good, because bringing them to the Work means bringing them to God. You are 24 BLESSED JOSEMARIA ESCRIVA fulfilling your duty as apostles. You are making our apostolate more effective, and above all, you are making me very happy.

## PERSONAL HOLINESS

### 24 {#inedito.alone-with-god.24}

My children, we who are children of God in Opus Dei should have just one exclusive concern, which is this: to become saints.

### 25 {#inedito.alone-with-god.25}

Holiness does not consist of doing more difficult things every day, but doing things with greater love every day.

### 26 {#inedito.alone-with-god.26}

If you want to be happy, be holy. If you want to be even happier, be more holy. If you want to be really happy, be very holy.

### 27 {#inedito.alone-with-god.27}

Holiness without joy is not the holiness of Opus Dei; holiness without doctrine is not the holiness of Opus Dei.

26 BLESSED JOSEMARIA ESCRIVA

### 28 {#inedito.alone-with-god.28}

My children have to be devout, learned, cheerful and... shrewd: holy and shrewd.

### 29 {#inedito.alone-with-god.29}

In the Work, everything is a means of holiness: work and relaxation; our life of devotion and the joys of living together; happiness and pain. In a word, every moment in our life contains a possibility for sanctification: in all things we ought to love and fulfil God’s Will.

### 30 {#inedito.alone-with-god.30}

Things can’t be improvised. Neither can holiness. God awaits us at every moment of the day. Souls continue to mature with the passage of time, as long as they don’t raise obstacles through their infidelity.

### 31 {#inedito.alone-with-god.31}

Our mission is to make the path of holiness in the world joyful and attractive.

### 32 {#inedito.alone-with-god.32}

Piety – piety – piety. If you act uncharitably, it will be because you lack interior life, and not because you have an awkward character.

### 33 {#inedito.alone-with-god.33}

The secret of our effectiveness is to be pious, sincerely devout.

### 34 {#inedito.alone-with-god.34}

The Norms have to be done punctually: hodie, nunc; faithfully, with loving care. And when we foresee difficulties, we don’t leave the Norms till later: we bring them forward.

### 35 {#inedito.alone-with-god.35}

I’m not interested in any enterprise, any apostolate, if my children are not improved by doing it. I measure the effectiveness and value of undertakings by the degree of holiness attained by the people involved in them.

### 36 {#inedito.alone-with-god.36}

Don’t forget, my children, that in Opus Dei it’s not enough to be good, or even very good; we have to be heroic.

### 37 {#inedito.alone-with-god.37}

I think that in heaven my glory will consist of seeing that my children are higher up than me. Don’t let me down: be genuine saints.

## FORMATION

### 38 {#inedito.alone-with-god.38}

Holiness and apostolate: these are the goals we seek,

individually and corporately. To attain these goals we need formation... through the appropriate means. Never expect to get special illuminations from God. He has no reason to provide them, when we already have the means to hand if we make the effort to use them.

### 39 {#inedito.alone-with-god.39}

Confusion, lack of faith, and continuous desertions are to

be seen in the ranks of God’s earthly army. It’s important not to forget, my children, that a campaign of atrocious propaganda is being waged against the holiness of marriage, the holiness of the priesthood, chastity and virginity, Mary the Mother of God and our Mother, the Holy Father, and the Blessed Eucharist.

30 BLESSED JOSEMARIA ESCRIVA

### 40 {#inedito.alone-with-god.40}

You have to feel the need and the joyful duty to ensure that the doctrine you receive, the formation you are given, is passed on to other minds and other hearts too, producing noble deeds as its fruit.

### 41 {#inedito.alone-with-god.41}

I have learned how to wait, which is a high degree of knowledge. God uses the passage of time to form souls. They cannot be forced, any more than a plant is normally forced to bloom more quickly.

### 42 {#inedito.alone-with-god.42}

My children, may it be possible to say of you what was said of our Lord: coepit facere et docere – Jesus began to do and teach (Acts 1:1). First he did things and afterwards he taught them.

### 43 {#inedito.alone-with-god.43}

Their own professional studies can never seem unimportant to members of Opus Dei. None of my children, if they have the right spirit, can have a casual approach to their studies, nor should they stand aloof from the hotly-debated issues of student life.

### 44 {#inedito.alone-with-god.44}

We need to be constantly fresh, to be able to say the same thing every day with new attractiveness. It is the gift of tongues, part of the gift of tongues.

### 45 {#inedito.alone-with-god.45}

Together with grace and charity, the soul also receives the gifts of the Holy Spirit. I would like charity to lead you to desire and ask for something which is not a gift of the Holy

Spirit, but is a fruit of charity: the gift of getting on with people.

### 46 {#inedito.alone-with-god.46}

Our system of teaching is made up of positive statements, not negative ones, and boils down to just two things: acting with common sense and supernatural sense.

### 47 {#inedito.alone-with-god.47}

Don’t forget that it is the weakness of good people that lends strength to bad ones. Therefore don’t ever fail to exercise your rights, while directing all your activity to the glory of God.

### 48 {#inedito.alone-with-god.48}

I have told you that we must have the faith of children and the doctrine of theologians. Now I add: and the piety of an old lady, sighing for love in the corner of a church. She doesn’t use big words or pseudo-scientific theories, but her devotion has tradition and maturity, like good wine.

### 49 {#inedito.alone-with-god.49}

We children of God in his Work have to be robust in our chastity, sturdily devout, apostolically hard-working, solid and practical in our devotion, active and prudent in our zeal, prompt and cordial in our obedience.

### 50 {#inedito.alone-with-god.50}

It’s not enough to want to be poor. One has to learn to be poor.

### 51 {#inedito.alone-with-god.51}

The wealth of Opus Dei is to be able to live in poverty.

### 52 {#inedito.alone-with-god.52}

To give after death is to be generous with what doesn’t

belong to one. Tell that to those well-endowed people who are reluctant to support apostolic enterprises except in their wills.

### 53 {#inedito.alone-with-god.53}

I bless with all my soul all those people who, with their

prayer, their work or their financial contributions, support the great supernatural enterprise of the Roman College of the Holy Cross.

### 54 {#inedito.alone-with-god.54}

Don’t forget, my son, my daughter, that you are

contributing to the formation of your brothers and sisters, and all souls, all the time: when working and when relaxing; when you look happy or when you look concerned; when you pray as a child of God in the middle of the street and the peace of your soul shows through on the outside; and when people can see that you’ve been crying, and you smile.

## WORKING FOR LOVE

### 55 {#inedito.alone-with-god.55}

A particular characteristic of the spirituality of Opus Dei

is that we each have to sanctify our job, our ordinary work; we have to sanctify ourselves in our job; and we have to sanctify others with our job.

### 56 {#inedito.alone-with-god.56}

For us work is the hinge on which our whole effort to

achieve Christian perfection turns. And in seeking Christian perfection in the middle of the world, we also necessarily have to seek human perfection in our own daily work. Which means that people who don’t do all they can to avoid shoddy work will never be able to understand us.

### 57 {#inedito.alone-with-god.57}

The members of God’s family don’t work because we

are paid to, like mercenaries. We do as much work as we can, like good sons and daughters.

### 58 {#inedito.alone-with-god.58}

Work. We work many hours a day, conscious of the

needs, including the financial ones, of the supernatural family we belong to. This is what it means to have a sense of responsibility, to want to be saints and serve God.

### 59 {#inedito.alone-with-god.59}

Your life and mine have to be simply ordinary. Every

day we try to finish the things we are obliged to do, and finish them well, avoiding shoddiness, complications or silly fantasies. God is always waiting for us: for me, among papers; and for you, wherever you happen to be... Our Lord is present in your books, in your laboratory apparatus, in the machines of the workshop or factory, in whatever professional work you have to carry out.

### 60 {#inedito.alone-with-god.60}

The best way to be a missionary, with a mission, but

without ever calling yourself a missionary, is to live a holy and happy life; and to do that you have to fulfil your social and professional duties well and to be always extremely charitable.

### 61 {#inedito.alone-with-god.61}

From the beginning I taught you that there is no such

thing as great or small jobs: they are all great if they are done for Love. Things which are seemingly small become great through the prism of our vocation of service.

### 62 {#inedito.alone-with-god.62}

In the service of God there are no positions of little importance. All are of great importance. The importance of a position depends on the individual carrying it out.

### 63 {#inedito.alone-with-god.63}

The best spirit of sacrifice is to maintain one’s enthusiasm for a task one has started on.

### 64 {#inedito.alone-with-god.64}

For a soul in love, work is a vigil, and so is relaxation. We have to finish the day with the loving thought: “Tomorrow, Lord, I will continue working for You”. And on waking up, the first word that comes to our heart should be an act of love for God and a renewal of our real desire to get down to work.

### 65 {#inedito.alone-with-god.65}

May your day be full of devotion and work. Because Opus Dei is operatio Dei, God’s work, and at the same time it is continual prayer. We are Opus Dei because for us life, and work, are always prayer, at all the cross-roads of the earth.

### 66 {#inedito.alone-with-god.66}

In carrying out their work, the children of God in his Opus Dei try not merely to fulfil, but to love, which always means surpassing themselves gladly in duty and sacrifice.

### 67 {#inedito.alone-with-god.67}

Work, work, work, optimistically. This is the great miracle we perform. Like that, no day is like the next, even though we do the same thing every day.

### 68 {#inedito.alone-with-god.68}

There comes a time, my children, when one counts the days that are left and feels the need to leave more work completed; not out of pride, but out of Love.

## CONTEMPLATIVE DIALOGUE

### 69 {#inedito.alone-with-god.69}

Our life as God’s children in his Opus Dei leads us to be present in all the hurly-burly of the world, and at the same time to be as recollected as if we were in the oratory.

### 70 {#inedito.alone-with-god.70}

To be with Christ is to be safe. To be able to contemplate oneself in Christ is to be able to be better every day. To get to know Christ is necessarily to love Christ. And to love Christ is to assure one’s happiness.

### 71 {#inedito.alone-with-god.71}

Our cell is the street. And so in the street (that is, the office, the studio, the university, the laboratory, the factory, or on the land) we must live in constant union with God. That is the only way to bring our environment, the bonus odor Christi, the aroma of Christ (cf. 2 Cor 2:15), to all walks of life. That is

the only way to be temperate in the use of earthly possessions – to practise a smiling asceticism. That is the only way to make our way loved.

### 72 {#inedito.alone-with-god.72}

Being a contemplative requires constant prayer; and

unless you are a contemplative it is impossible to persevere in Opus Dei.

### 73 {#inedito.alone-with-god.73}

Is it right that our Lord, being who he is, is always

attentive to you, while you, being who you are, forget about him so often?

### 74 {#inedito.alone-with-god.74}

We have to be forever focused on our Lord, at every

moment; otherwise we are a hindrance in the Work and could even end up losing our vocation. Don’t seek excuses in sickness, or old age, or work: we have always made use of all those circumstances as means of sanctification. That way we will be friendly towards everybody, and happy here on earth, with the relative happiness that can be achieved here below. But we will never feel unhappy, even though our heart aches or tears spring to our eyes.

### 75 {#inedito.alone-with-god.75}

Am I really more concerned about trifles and petty

details, which merit no attention and from which I can expect nothing, than I am about my God? Whom am I with, when I’m not with God?

### 76 {#inedito.alone-with-god.76}

On feeling our responsibility to make the Work grow –

and what a beautiful responsibility it is! – we are driven to seek

the support of prayer, an uninterrupted dialogue with our Lord. “Without you, my God, I can do nothing; with you, I can do everything.”

### 77 {#inedito.alone-with-god.77}

First, devotion. If we are devout, we will necessarily do

our duty, because when devotion is genuine, it leads us to work and fulfil our daily duties willingly. That’s why we are contemplatives, because the supernatural inner basis of human action is closely linked to its external expression.

### 78 {#inedito.alone-with-god.78}

Be truly devout, strive to maintain a continuous presence

of God, and then you will not lose the supernatural meaning of your lives. The love that led you to give yourselves will be forever youthful, as will your zeal for souls, which is its necessary consequence. My daughters and sons, bourgeois mentality, and a lack of zeal and vibrancy, are all forms of disloyalty to God. And bourgeois types do us harm: they hinder the whole enterprise.

### 79 {#inedito.alone-with-god.79}

For us prayer is not a word game. It has to have practical

expressions: improvement, progress in our personal dedication to Christ our Lord.

### 80 {#inedito.alone-with-god.80}

What confidence prayer must give you, my children!

Praying, praying is the system; and then setting to work calmly and cheerfully.

### 81 {#inedito.alone-with-god.81}

In these moments of trial for our Holy Mother the

Church we have to pray continually to our Lord – clama, ne

cesses! Cry aloud, spare not! (Is 58:1) – impatiently and urgently. Holy impatience (not panic, nor anxiety, nor selfishness) is an expression of supernatural zeal. And we will realise it is holy if, at the same time as we plead with our Lord, we feel the reproach that we can and should give ourselves more, and pray more continually.

### 82 {#inedito.alone-with-god.82}

Live your Christian life naturally. Reflect Christ like a

plain, clear mirror that doesn’t distort and produce a caricature. If you are plain and clear, like that mirror, you will reflect Christ’s life and you will pass it on to others.

### 83 {#inedito.alone-with-god.83}

Opus Dei is the fruit of prayer, and only through prayer

will it continue to make progress. That is why I keep insisting that you must be very devout, without being afraid to let it show. Beg God to increase your devotion each day and to make it deeper, stronger and more sincere.

## HUMBLE INSTRUMENTS

### 84 {#inedito.alone-with-god.84}

Hiding and disappearing is a skill the world doesn’t

understand. It is the wisdom of heaven, and a way to happiness on earth.

### 85 {#inedito.alone-with-god.85}

I send you forth throughout the world with a strong sense

of confidence, a “superiority complex,” since it is God our Lord who wants you to do this apostolate. Be convinced of it. Pray about the things I am telling you now. ### 301 {#inedito.alone-with-god.301}

Forward, joyfully, determinedly. No-one in the world can stop us. To those who love God everything is good. In life everything can be put right, everything except death, and for us death is Life.

### 302 {#inedito.alone-with-god.302}

We have to be very happy: everything will be fine. Things have always worked out, even when we caused obstacles ourselves, through our infidelity.

### 303 {#inedito.alone-with-god.303}

Forward, without worrying about misunderstandings (which will disappear with time) or a lack of vision on the part of some people who ought to know better. You need to have peace, serenity, charity, and, on the human plane, the ability to keep quiet and smile cheerfully.

### 304 {#inedito.alone-with-god.304}

Always be serene, full of peace and joy in Domino, in

the Lord; because every difficulty is overcome when your only wish is to serve the Church and souls.

### 305 {#inedito.alone-with-god.305}

I want to give my children the secret of happiness. In Opus Dei we have to be happy and cheerful, and that is attained by being faithful. Being faithful in the tiniest aspects of our interior life, and being docile in sincerity and obedience. That’s how to be happy.

### 306 {#inedito.alone-with-god.306}

You ask me for a motto for the New Year, and I’ll

give you the same one as always: God’s grace and good humour.

## FIDELITY

### 307 {#inedito.alone-with-god.307}

I believe... that’s a word I rarely use. I believe in few things, just the minimum, but I believe in that with all my heart. And among the things I believe in are your faithfulness, your struggle, your ups and downs... which are always lived through in God’s sight. I don’t believe in myself at all. I believe in you; you are one of the reasons for my faithfulness. Not the main one, because the main one is my love for Jesus Christ, God the Father, God the Holy Spirit and our Blessed Lady.

### 308 {#inedito.alone-with-god.308}

When I think of you, each of you, every day, I think of

how you are Opus Dei, and my heart overflows with joy and thanksgiving, because I know you’re going to be faithful.

### 309 {#inedito.alone-with-god.309}

To think that through your infidelity and mine we can

make the apostolate less effective!

### 310 {#inedito.alone-with-god.310}

Be faithful! I know you are, my children. I’m saying

this for twenty, thirty, forty years hence. My children, be faithful, be faithful: that’s the way to be happy, joyful and effective.

### 311 {#inedito.alone-with-god.311}

If you and I are faithful, how far we can each get!

### 312 {#inedito.alone-with-god.312}

I am certain that these next five years are going to be very fruitful, if you are faithful. And to be faithful, just fulfil the Norms.*

### 313 {#inedito.alone-with-god.313}

To be faithful, you have God’s grace and the

tremendous help of your call, your vocation. How would you feel towards someone who told you, “I’m at your service”? You’d be grateful to them, wouldn’t you? Well that is just what we have done for God our Lord: placed ourselves at his service. My children, God never lets us outdo him in generosity, and he grants fidelity to those who surrender themselves to him.

### 314 {#inedito.alone-with-god.314}

If you are faithful you will be able to boast of victory, and in your life you will not experience defeat. There is no such thing as failure as long as we work with a pure intention and the desire to fulfil God’s Will. Whether or not we are

* At the closure of a General Congress of the Women’s Section

successful, we have still triumphed, because we have done our work for Love.

### 315 {#inedito.alone-with-god.315}

You know, don’t you, the normal meaning of loyalty,

not only in military life, but in civil life too? Well, place yourselves in God’s presence and think what it means to be loyal in the supernatural order. Perseverance, my children, is loyalty towards God.

### 316 {#inedito.alone-with-god.316}

My children, inculcate a sense of loyalty in people’s

minds and hearts. This exquisite Christian charity is something practically unknown, even among people who call themselves Christians. I can see that your own lives are indeed filled full of it, but it has to be put in the first place, as a human virtue and a supernatural one, when forming the young people of the Work.

### 317 {#inedito.alone-with-god.317}

Be faithful. Be loyal, loyal, loyal! That’s what it’s

called in civil life: loyalty. Ask our Lord to make me good and faithful, good and faithful: loyal. I’m asking God to do the same for you as well.

### 318 {#inedito.alone-with-god.318}

Fidelity in human terms is called loyalty. Be loyal.

Over and above the obstacles, or opposition, or all circumstances of illness or health. You will achieve wonders on the basis of fidelity and loyalty.

### 319 {#inedito.alone-with-god.319}

Commitment! How I like that word! We freely

commit ourselves to live completely dedicated to our Lord. We want him to reign over us completely and utterly. It may be

difficult to live up to our commitment, but even then fidelity is a joyful obligation that we must not try and get out of, even though it means giving up everything, even though it entails sacrifice and effort. God needs our fidelity.

### 320 {#inedito.alone-with-god.320}

You will persevere if you are devout, if you say

aspirations, if you are centred on our Lord. Interior life – piety

– is essential for perseverance. You will be pious if your relationship with the Heart of Christ and the Heart of our Blessed Mother is one of continuous prayer.

### 321 {#inedito.alone-with-god.321}

If you are faithful, when the years have gone by and you are old, you will feel a paternal tenderness towards the young people around you, because, through God’s grace, your “yes” will have been fruitful.

### 322 {#inedito.alone-with-god.322}

Feel the responsibility you have for your mission: all

Heaven is watching you.

### 323 {#inedito.alone-with-god.323}

The style and attractiveness of the Work is very much

your responsibility. It depends to a large extent on you, and on those who come after you.

### 324 {#inedito.alone-with-god.324}

Doesn’t it make you very happy to see that fidelity

depends to a great extent on us? I am excited at the thought that God loves me and has wanted his Work to depend on my response as well. And it makes me happy to be able to tell him, freely, “Lord, I love you too; count on my littleness.”

### 325 {#inedito.alone-with-god.325}

Tell them to love our Blessed Mother truly and to

imitate her; to talk to her and listen to her. As always, tell them from me not to complicate their lives with unimportant little things, but to forget them at once, having offered them cheerfully to our Lord. And so they will be simple, humble, optimistic, and effective; souls of prayer and sacrifice, according to the spirit of our Work.

## OUR MOTHER

### 326 {#inedito.alone-with-god.326}

Piety improves with practice. By being pious, you will

become more pious every day; by being devoted to our Lady, your devotion will grow stronger every day. You learn to walk by walking. You learn to have true piety, true devotion to our Lady, by starting to have devotion to her. Bring that love to life, make it more supernatural. Don’t turn to our Lady only to ask for things. Go to give her things too: to give her affection, to offer her your love for her divine Son, to express your affection in deeds of service to other people.

### 327 {#inedito.alone-with-god.327}

Our Opus Dei was born and has grown under our

Lady’s mantle. That’s why we, children of God in this Work of

God, have all those Marian customs spread through our daily lives. You can imagine how happy I was when I heard that the Roman Pontiff had consecrated the year 1954 to our Blessed Lady. For our part, we will respond to the Holy Father’s wishes by renewing our Marian devotions with greater love than ever, if possible. And also by undertaking the duty to spread devotion to the Rosary particularly this year; and by making three visits to shrines of our Lady, according to our custom: one in February, one in May and the final one in October. May our Immaculate Mother always be our joy and our light.*

### 328 {#inedito.alone-with-god.328}

Let us invoke our Lady the Virgin. May that good

Mother of ours, who is so powerful before her Son, remember us: Monstra te esse matrem! Show that you are our mother!

### 329 {#inedito.alone-with-god.329}

To say the Holy Rosary, considering the mysteries,

repeating the Our Father and Hail Mary, with the praises to the Blessed Trinity and the constant invocation of the Mother of God, is a continuous act of faith, hope and love, of adoration and reparation.

### 330 {#inedito.alone-with-god.330}

I have taught you to make use of distractions in prayer to continue talking with God; do the same when saying the Rosary. If you get distracted in spite of your best intentions, don’t worry. Keep praying, because that prayer is like the sound of a

* At the inauguration of the Marian Year in the universal Church in 1954

lover’s guitar as he serenades his beloved. Although your thoughts are elsewhere, your good desires and your vocal prayer are present there before our Lord and his Mother, in a song of love.

### 331 {#inedito.alone-with-god.331}

When I am contemplating the joyful mysteries of the

Rosary, and also at other times of the day, I focus on Joseph almost without meaning to. Don’t separate Joseph from our Blessed Lady: and so, with both of them, you will get to Jesus, you will learn to love God’s holy Will, no matter what it costs, and you will free yourselves more and more from the little bonds that up to then you may not have discovered.

### 332 {#inedito.alone-with-god.332}

My beloved daughters and sons, you will have

wondered why I have been travelling from one Marian shrine to another in many countries during these last years, in a continual pilgrimage, which also gives me an opportunity to thank our Lord for the chance to meet thousands of his daughters and sons in Opus Dei. What is the Father asking for? Well, at the feet of our holy Mother Mary, the all-powerful supplicant, the Father is praying for peace in the world, and holiness for the Church, for the Work and for each of his daughters and sons. Pray in close union with my prayer, and especially with the intentions of my Mass. Like that you will share in the blessed unity of the whole Work, and you will get the strength to persevere at our wonderful work of sowing peace and joy throughout the length and breadth of creation, with ever greater love and sacrifice.

### 333 {#inedito.alone-with-god.333}

Invoke our Blessed Lady. Don’t stop asking her to be always our Mother – monstra te esse Matrem! – and to give us, along with her Son’s grace, good clear doctrine in our minds and love and purity in our hearts, so that we can get to God and lead many souls to him.

### 334 {#inedito.alone-with-god.334}

Holy Mary, O good Mother, whenever we need it,

may we hear your gentle reproach at the bottom of our hearts, telling us that time doesn’t belong to us, because it belongs to our heavenly Father.

### 335 {#inedito.alone-with-god.335}

Ecce Ancilla. Behold the handmaid. Out of love for

our Lady and to remind us of the power of our heavenly Mother, who with a few words made the Redemption possible, I have placed constantly on my daughters’ lips the aspiration Sancta Maria, Spes nostra, Ancilla Domini. When you see yourself as God’s servant, God’s handmaid, you will have the same spirit as your heavenly Mother, you will love God’s grace, and will be ambitious to do great things on earth on the basis of little things.

### 336 {#inedito.alone-with-god.336}

Of late we have been entrusting some very important

issues to St Joseph, our Father and Lord. During May, as you go on pilgrimage with Mary and Joseph, let’s go to Jesus; and then you’ll see how quickly our wishes come true.

### 337 {#inedito.alone-with-god.337}

In memory of the consecration of Opus Dei to our Blessed Mother, and so that there may always be souls who seek her protection, it is my wish that, in both Sections of the

Work, many times a day, there should arise from our hearts the cry Cor Mariae Dulcissimum, iter para tutum! Most sweet Heart of Mary, prepare a safe way!*
